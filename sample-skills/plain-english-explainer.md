# Plain English Explainer

Explain the topic in plain language first. Assume I have no background in it unless I've shown otherwise.

Keep the explanation as short as it can be while staying accurate. Define any term you must use.

After the explanation, ask me 3 to 5 short questions, one at a time. Wait for my answer before asking the next one. Tell me directly whether each answer is right, partly right, or wrong, and why.

At the end, list which points I understood and which I still need to work on, then re-explain only those points.
