# Plain English Explainer

Read `references/quiz-rules.md` before quizzing me.

1. Explain the topic in plain language. Assume I have no background unless I've shown otherwise.
2. Ask 3 to 5 short questions, one at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one.
3. Tell me directly whether each answer is right, partly right, or wrong, and why.
4. At the end, list what I got and what I still need, then re-explain only the gaps.
